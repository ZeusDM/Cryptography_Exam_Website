  "raw_statement": "Let $G$ be a (cyclic) group of prime order $n > 2$, and let $\\alpha$ be a generator of $G$. We assume that the group operation in $G$ can be computed in polynomial time. Recall that the Diffie-Hellman problem for $G$ with respect to $\\alpha$ (DHP$_\\alpha$) is the following: given $\\alpha^a$ and $\\alpha^b$, compute $\\alpha^{ab}$. The Decisional Diffie-Hellman problem for $G$ with respect to $\\alpha$ (DDHP$_\\alpha$) is the following: given $\\alpha^a$, $\\alpha^b$, and $\\alpha^c$, decide whether $c \\equiv ab \\pmod{n}$.\n\nIn the basic ElGamal public-key encryption scheme, Alice's private key is an integer $a \\in [1, n-1]$, and her public key is $\\beta = \\alpha^a$. To encrypt a plaintext message $m \\in G$ for Alice, Bob selects $k \\in_R [1, n-1]$, and sends the ciphertext $C = (\\alpha^k, m \\cdot \\beta^k)$ to Alice.\n\nIn the following, we consider ciphertext-only attacks on the basic ElGamal public-key encryption scheme. The attacker has knowledge of the group parameters, Alice's public key $\\beta$, and one or more ciphertexts.\n\n\\begin{enumerate}[label=(\\alph*)]\n\\item The ElGamal-decrypt problem is the following: Given a public key $\\beta$ and a ciphertext $C$, compute the corresponding plaintext. Prove that the ElGamal-decrypt problem is polynomial-time equivalent to DHP$_\\alpha$.\n\n\\item Prove that the semantic security of the basic ElGamal public-key encryption scheme (under ciphertext-only attack) is polynomial-time equivalent to DDHP$_\\alpha$.\n\n\\item Is the basic ElGamal public-key encryption scheme semantically secure against chosen-plaintext attacks? (Justify your answer.)\n\\end{enumerate}",
