  "raw_statement": "Let \\(G\\) be a cyclic group of prime order \\(n > 2\\) generated by \\(\\alpha\\). The notation \\(A \\leq_P B\\) means that problem \\(A\\) polynomial-time reduces to problem \\(B\\).\n\n\\begin{enumerate}[label=(\\alph*)]\n    \\item Recall that the discrete logarithm problem in \\(G\\) with respect to \\(\\alpha\\) (\\(\\mathrm{DLP}_\\alpha\\)) is the following: given \\(\\gamma \\in G\\), find the integer \\(\\ell \\in [0, n-1]\\) that satisfies \\(\\gamma = \\alpha^\\ell\\). Now, let \\(\\beta\\) be another generator of \\(G\\). Prove that \\(\\mathrm{DLP}_\\alpha \\leq_P \\mathrm{DLP}_\\beta\\). (This proves that the hardness of the DLP does not depend on the choice of generator.)\n\n    \\item Recall that the Diffie-Hellman Problem (\\(\\mathrm{DHP}\\)) is the following: given \\(\\alpha^x, \\alpha^y \\in G\\), compute \\(\\alpha^{xy}\\). The problem \\(\\mathrm{INV}\\) is the following: given \\(\\alpha^x \\in G\\), compute \\(\\alpha^{x^{-1}}\\) (where \\(x^{-1}\\) is the inverse of \\(x\\) modulo \\(n\\)). Prove that \\(\\mathrm{INV} \\leq_P \\mathrm{DHP}\\).\n\n    \\item The problem \\(\\mathrm{SQUARE}\\) is the following: given \\(\\alpha^x \\in G\\), compute \\(\\alpha^{x^2}\\). Prove that \\(\\mathrm{DHP} \\leq_P \\mathrm{SQUARE}\\).\n\n    \\item The problem \\(\\mathrm{CUBE}\\) is the following: given \\(\\alpha^x \\in G\\), compute \\(\\alpha^{x^3}\\). Prove that \\(\\mathrm{CUBE} \\leq_P \\mathrm{SQUARE}\\).\n\n    \\item Prove that \\(\\mathrm{DHP} \\leq_P \\mathrm{CUBE}\\). (Hint: Recall that \\((x+1)^3 = x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 1\\).)\n\\end{enumerate}",
